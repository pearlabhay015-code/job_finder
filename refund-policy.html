Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Application fee

The first successfully submitted job application on an account is free. Each later application costs ₹200.00, unless another amount is clearly displayed before checkout. The fee is charged only after you choose to continue to payment.

When a refund may be requested

Contact us if any of the following happens:

  • Your payment was charged more than once for the same application.
  • Your bank or payment provider shows a debit, but the payment failed or the application was not confirmed.
  • A technical issue prevented a paid application from being submitted after payment.

Once a paid application has been successfully submitted, the application fee is generally not refundable. This does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.

How to request a refund

Email myappointers@gmail.com with the email address on your account, job/application ID, payment date, amount, and Razorpay payment ID if available. Please do not email your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or banking password.

Review and processing time

We will review the transaction and, if a refund is approved, initiate it to the original payment method within 7 business days. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the credit. We will contact you if we need more information.

Payments are processed through Razorpay. A payment marked as pending may take time to update; contact us if it remains unresolved or your bank confirms a debit.

Contact

For payment or application help, email myappointers@gmail.com, call 9060717287, or visit our Contact Us page.
